## Press copy

Three lengths — grab whichever fits your format.

### Boilerplate (one line)
> FridgeCig is a playful iOS app that lets Diet Coke and soda superfans track their daily intake with streaks, 70+ badges, collectible state cans, and friends.

### Short (~50 words)
> FridgeCig turns your soda habit into a game. Log every Diet Coke, Coke Zero, or fountain drink to build daily streaks, unlock 70+ hilarious badges, collect a can from every U.S. state, and compete with friends on the leaderboard. It's not a hydration app — it's for people who love their fridge cig.

### Long (~150 words)
> FridgeCig is the soda tracker for people who are *proud* of their habit. Where other apps nag you about water, FridgeCig celebrates the daily ritual of cracking open a cold Diet Coke.
>
> Log any of 17 drink types — cans, bottles, fountain, and fast-food sizes — to build a daily streak, then unlock 70+ badges spanning milestones, volume, variety, and gloriously specific lifestyle achievements. Collectors can chase a limited-edition can from all 50 states via an interactive U.S. map, plus 20+ special editions including FIFA 2026 and Coca-Cola Creations.
>
> It's social, too: add friends, climb leaderboards, react with cheers, and share themed Weekly Recap cards. An Apple Watch app and widgets make logging a one-tap affair, and everything syncs over iCloud. FridgeCig Pro unlocks premium themes like Midnight, Neon, Forest, and Sunset.
>
> We don't judge. We just track.

## Key features

- **Track your Diet Coke streak** — one-tap logging across 17 drink types builds a daily streak.
- **Unlock 70+ hilarious badges** — milestones, streaks, volume, variety, special editions, and lifestyle achievements.
- **Collect every state can** — chase the America 250 limited-edition cans from all 50 states on an interactive U.S. map.
- **Compete with friends** — friend codes, leaderboards, an activity feed, and cheers.
- **Share your Weekly Recap** — themed, stickered recap cards exportable to Photos.
- **Apple Watch + widgets** — log from your wrist, Home Screen, or Lock Screen.
- **20+ special editions** — FIFA 2026, Coca-Cola Creations, and more.
- **iCloud sync** — your collection follows you across devices.

## Positioning

- **What it is:** a niche, self-aware lifestyle app celebrating soda culture.
- **What it is *not*:** a health or hydration tracker. No guilt, no nagging.
- **Audience:** Diet Coke / Coke Zero superfans, 18–35, social-media active (TikTok-first), community-minded and in on the joke.
- **Voice:** celebratory, funny, non-judgmental — *"We don't judge, we just track."*
