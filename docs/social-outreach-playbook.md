# FridgeCig Outreach Playbook

How to find people already talking about FridgeCig or their Diet Coke love, and
turn them into users, posters, and creator partners. Companion to
[social-media-content-strategy.md](social-media-content-strategy.md) (what to
post) — this doc is who to talk to and what to say.

**The one rule:** every comment, DM, and email is sent by a human (you). No DM
automation, no mass sends — it violates every platform's ToS, gets the account
flagged, and reads as spam anyway. What we automate is the *finding* and the
*drafting*; the send is always manual.

## 2. The engagement ladder

Work bottom-up. Most accounts never need a DM — a great comment from the
FridgeCig account *is* the outreach, and it's public reach.

1. **React + comment** (default, unlimited) — be the funniest reply, not an ad.
   Mention the app only when it's genuinely the punchline.
2. **Reply with the app when invited** — someone asks "is there an app for
   this?" or jokes about tracking: now the link/screenshot is the *answer*, not
   a plug.
3. **DM / email** (rationed: ≤5–10/day, only with a reason) — creator seeding
   offer, repost permission, or thanking someone who mentioned FridgeCig
   unprompted.
4. **Repost/feature** — with permission, feature their post ("Featured Sipper").
   People promote apps that promote them.

### Comment starters (rewrite per post, never paste verbatim into multiple posts)
- On a DC haul: "This cart is a personality and we support it."
- On a "first DC of the day" post: "6am fridge cig is the most powerful move in the game."
- On someone counting their cans: "You're doing manually what we built an entire app for. Respect."
- On a McDonald's DC take: "McDonald's DC being its own food group is canon in our app, for the record."
- Invited ("someone should make an app"): "We did. Streaks, badges, a leaderboard. It's called FridgeCig — come get your flowers."

## 3. Message templates

Personalize the first line of every send — reference *their* specific post.
A template with no personalization is spam with extra steps.

### A. Creator seeding DM (TikTok/IG)
> Hey [name] — your [specific video, e.g. "gas station fountain ranking"] was
> perfect. I'm the (solo) dev behind FridgeCig, an app that turns Diet Coke
> devotion into streaks, badges, and friend leaderboards. Can I send you a free
> lifetime Pro code? Zero obligation to post — I just think you'd genuinely
> enjoy your weekly recap card. 🥤

### B. Follow-up (once, ~5 days later, then drop it)
> No pressure at all — code's here whenever you want it: [CODE]. Keep making
> the good stuff either way. 🥤

### C. Someone mentioned FridgeCig unprompted
> This made my week — I built FridgeCig solo, so seeing it out in the wild is
> surreal. Mind if I repost this to our page? And DM me your username, there
> may be a badge in it for you.

### D. Email (for creators with a business email / management)
> Subject: Free FridgeCig Pro for [name] — from the dev
>
> Hi [name], I'm Brevin, the solo developer of FridgeCig
> (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757887069) — an iOS app that tracks Diet Coke
> habits with streaks, 70+ badges, and friend leaderboards. Your DC content is
> exactly the energy the app is built on, so I'd love to send you lifetime Pro,
> no strings attached. If you ever felt like sharing your weekly recap card or
> badge collection, amazing — but the code is yours either way.
>
> Press kit: https://brevinb.github.io/FridgeCig/press-kit
> — Brevin

### E. Reddit (r/DietCoke etc.)
Never template Reddit. Participate normally for a while, then one honest post:
"I built an app to track my Diet Coke habit. It has badges." — screenshots,
the story, answer every comment. Check each sub's self-promo rules first.

**Pro codes:** generate one-time offer codes in App Store Connect → your app →
Monetization → Subscriptions → Offer Codes. Give each creator their own code so
the tracker shows who redeemed.

## 4. Tracker

Log every outbound touch in [`social-tools/outreach-tracker.csv`](../social-tools/outreach-tracker.csv)
— platform, handle, what they posted, what you did, code given, status. Rules:

- One follow-up max. No reply after that = closed, stay friendly in comments.
- Don't re-DM an account within 60 days.
- Review weekly: who redeemed, who posted, who's worth a deeper partnership.

## 5. Weekly rhythm

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Brand-mention searches; reply to every mention | 5 min |
| Mon/Wed/Fri | DC-culture sweep; leave 5–10 real comments | 10 min |
| Weekly (pick a day) | Creator scouting; send 3–5 seeding DMs; update tracker | 30 min |
| Weekly | Feature one community post ("Featured Sipper") | 10 min |
