## Press release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### FridgeCig 2.5 gives Diet Coke fans a global photo feed

*The soda-tracking app for self-described "fridge cig" enthusiasts adds photo
posts, a public Global feed, and new holiday special editions.*

**[CITY], [DATE]** — FridgeCig, the iOS app that turns a Diet Coke habit into
streaks, badges, and friendly competition, today released version 2.5. The
update centers on photos: users can snap their drink as they log it and post
it to a public Global feed shared by FridgeCig users everywhere.

"People already photograph their Diet Cokes: the first can of the morning, the
perfect fountain pour, the fridge restock," said Brevin, FridgeCig's developer.
"2.5 gives those photos a home. It's a feed where every post is someone's
favorite drink."

**What's new in FridgeCig 2.5**

- **Snap Your Drink.** The camera is now the first thing in the log screen,
  and the drink details sit on top of the photo.
- **Post to Global.** Photos can be shared to a worldwide feed. Each post gets
  an explicit Global, Friends, or Only Me choice, and Global can be turned off
  entirely in Sharing Settings.
- **Holiday Creamy Vanilla and Orange Cream.** New special editions to log,
  each with a collectible badge.
- **Smarter streak reminders.** Reminders now reflect whether you've already
  logged today, so streaks are easier to protect.
- **Faster setup.** New users pick their usual drink and a community name, then
  log their first drink in a few taps.

FridgeCig also includes 70+ badges, friend leaderboards, Weekly Recap share
cards, America 250 state-can collecting, an Apple Watch app, and Home and Lock
Screen widgets.

**Pricing and availability**
FridgeCig 2.5 is available now as a free download on the App Store for iPhone
and Apple Watch. FridgeCig Pro, an optional upgrade, unlocks premium themes
and streak freezes.

**About FridgeCig**
FridgeCig is an independent iOS app for Diet Coke and soda superfans, built by
a fellow fan. Its motto: *We don't judge. We just track.*
